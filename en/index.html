The honest questions

"Is this really for me?"

I’m not technical. Do I need to know how to code?
No—and that’s the change of era. The first 4 modules use browser only. When the terminal appears (module 5), you already have 4 things working, learn with 6 commands, and have an alternative route that requires no installation. The AI writes the code; you give the instructions.
I don’t have time for another course.
Each lesson states how long it takes before you begin (25–40 min) and fits into a lunch break. And your “homework” is your own work: the exercises use your real tasks — the time invested comes back the same week.
I've used ChatGPT and nothing changed.
Because chat is conversation, and conversation doesn't change routines. Here, you build agents with a fixed mission + a knowledge base with your expertise. It’s the difference between asking a stranger for a favor and having an employee trained by you.
Won’t AI replace me?
The history of previous turning points has the answer: the computer didn’t replace people who learned Office—it replaced the typewriter. People who work with agents replace those who don’t. And in this program, the raw material for agents is YOUR experience — it becomes more valuable, not less.
I’m 40+ / 50+. Isn’t this for young people?
You’ve already made it through two technological shifts — computers and the internet. This is the third time, not the first. And this time you have what young people don’t: a profession, real problems, and experience to turn into agents. The program was designed for busy adults, with no jargon and no artificial rush.
What if something goes wrong on my computer? I get stuck with this stuff.
Errors are literally part of the method: before the first command, you learn to copy any error and paste it into the AI asking for help. Each technical exercise has a card that says “if this appears, do this” and a alternative route through the browser. No one gets stuck on a red screen.
I never finish online courses.
Because most end with "got it"—and understanding alone doesn't keep anyone on track. Here, every module ends with something of your own working, and the final project is just assembling what you’ve already built. You’re not chasing a certificate; you’re building a system you use every day.
Are my data and my clients' data safe?
This is lesson content, not a footer: every path has the block "what never to put in AI" (professional confidentiality, client and patient data, LGPD), and sensitive exercises use fictitious data. You learn to use AI with the responsibility your profession demands.
